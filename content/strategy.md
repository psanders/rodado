## Audience
Marketing managers at mid-size Dominican product brands that spend US$800–5,000 a month on marketing
(media, materials and other efforts). Rodado is one line in that budget.
- Not: solo sellers without budget. Not: giant brands with a big agency.
- Agencies: welcome if they come on their own, but we don't make content for them.
- Categories: food and drinks, beauty and personal care, consumer goods, retail with physical products.
- What they must believe: 1) it looks premium and it's their real product, 2) we deliver on time, 3) their boss will approve it.
- Objections: "AI looks fake", "my boss won't approve AI", "US$299 seems too cheap to be good".

## Goal
Qualified WhatsApp conversations (a brand with active Meta ads and a marketing budget ≥ US$800/month).

| Weeks | Target |
| --- | --- |
| 1–4 | 5 qualified conversations |
| 5–8 | 12 conversations, 3 first orders |
| 9–12 | 20 conversations, 6 orders, 2 brands ordering every month |

Attribution: bio link with the prefilled text "Hola, vengo de Instagram y quiero un anuncio para mi marca" + the keyword ANUNCIO in CTAs.

## Decisions (Oct 4, 2026)
- Price: stays at US$299. The client's budget covers all their marketing; we're one part of it.
- Monthly plan: defined once there's demand. Simple offer for now.
- Outbound: Pedro, 10+ messages a day to managers on the prospect list.
- Client work: can be shown with the logo replaced by an invented look-alike.
- Volume: 7 a week. If a week slips, Saturday goes first. Never Sunday.
- Website: update "Ideal para" later.
- Publishing: Meta Business Suite, one week at a time.

## Measurement
Per post: reach from non-followers, average watch time (Reels), saves + shares, profile visits, WhatsApp chats.
Rule: a format stays if it beats the account median on saves + shares or produces a chat within 4 weeks.
Cycle: weeks 1–4 explore (everything fixed), 5–8 double down on what wins, 9–12 add a channel (LinkedIn first).
