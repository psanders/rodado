# Idea brief

One idea = one brief. Same shape in `today.md`, `bank.md` and on WhatsApp.

## On WhatsApp (what Pedro sees)

```
*Idea 1 of 3 · <Day slot> · <Format name>*
*<Title, max 8 words>*

*Picture it:* <2–3 sentences: what the viewer sees and hears, first frame to last. Concrete: product, colors, text on screen, the turn.>

*Hook:* "<first line or first 2 s, in Spanish>"

*Where it comes from:* <source type> — <the concrete thing: link, project, the question someone asked, the date on the calendar>

*Why it works:* <1–2 sentences: the mechanism. Why a busy manager stops, saves or writes.>

*Why it fits Rodado:* <pillar + which belief or objection it moves, and why now (bank gap, calendar, last week's result)>

*Effort:* <low / medium / high — what we'd need>

Reply *sí*, *no* (and why, if you like), or a tweak.
```

Format names: Reel storyboard→ad · Carousel framework · Reel category concept · Carousel FAQ · Static one-liner · Reel offer.
Use "Idea 2 of 3" for the next approved slot, not the candidate number.

## In bank.md

```
## I019 · <Title>
- status: bank

- slot: tue · carousel-framework · strategy

- picture: <...>

- why it works: <...>

- effort: low
- score: 3
- notes: <Pedro's tweak or comment, if any>
```

## Quality bar (check before sending)
- Could Pedro picture the finished post from this alone? If not, the picture is too vague.
- Is the source something real you can point to? "Trends" is not a source; the specific post, ad, question or date is.
- Does the hook work with the sound off and in under 2 seconds?
- Would it make sense to a marketing manager in Santo Domingo, not to a marketer in general?
