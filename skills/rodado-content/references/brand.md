# Rodado brand reference

## Who we talk to
Marketing managers at mid-size Dominican product brands (food & drinks, beauty & personal care, consumer goods, retail) with US$800–5,000/month in total marketing budget. Not solo sellers; not giant brands with big agencies. Agencies are welcome but we don't write for them.
They must believe: 1) it looks premium and it's their real product, 2) we deliver on time, 3) their boss will approve it.
Objections to answer: "la IA se ve falsa", "mi jefe no aprueba IA", "US$299 parece muy barato para ser bueno".

## Goal of every post
Qualified WhatsApp conversations, not likes. Rank: qualified chats > saves + shares > reach.

## Offer facts (never change)
- US$299 per ad · one story edited in 3 durations: 30, 20 and 15 s · 1080 × 1920
- 2 creative directions in 48 h; second direction +US$60
- 7 business days of production · 2 revision rounds · 100% guarantee
- Built from photos of the client's real product; no shoot, no studio
- WhatsApp +1 (785) 317-8070 · keyword ANUNCIO · roda.do · @rodado.creativo

## Voice (copy is in Spanish)
- A creative director who respects a busy manager's time. Direct, confident, no hype. Short sentences. "Tú".
- Neutral Dominican Spanish. "gancho", not "hook"; "anuncio", not "ad".
- Talk about results on Meta (sales, tests, ROAS), not "AI magic". AI is the how, not the what.
- Use: anuncio · gancho · 3 duraciones · sin rodaje · tu producto real · listo para Meta · 7 días hábiles · garantía
- Avoid: revolucionario, increíble, viral garantizado, contenido orgánico, barato; max. 1 emoji per caption.

## Visual rules
- Fonts: Instrument Serif (titles), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (labels, timings, slide numbers).
- Colors: ink #16130F, paper #F5EFE4, sand #E9E0D1, record red #E23D2A, smoke #6E665B.
- Sizes: Reels and covers 1080 × 1920; carousels and statics 1080 × 1350 (4:5).
- Keep text out of Instagram's unsafe zones (9:16: 130 px top, 320 px bottom). Reel cover title inside the central 3:4 area.
- Small "roda.do" watermark in a bottom corner of every piece.
- File names: `YYYY-MM-DD-<format>-<topic>.<ext>`; carousel slides `-01.png … -08.png`.
