# Static · One-liner (open) · 1 slide · 4:5

Purpose: a strong, true, useful opinion a manager would share with their team. Spark comments and shares.

Layout: brand background (ink or paper), large Instrument Serif quote, "roda.do" signature. Max. 15 words.
Test: would a marketing manager forward it to their team? If not, rewrite.
Examples: "Si tu anuncio necesita sonido para entenderse, ya perdió." · "Tu anuncio no se cansó. Tu público se cansó de verlo." · "Una sesión de fotos no es una campaña."
Default CTA: Share.

Media: optional. One image or a 4–6 s clip behind the quote (calm lower third). Text-only statics alternate layouts (quote, statement with one highlighted word, big number).
