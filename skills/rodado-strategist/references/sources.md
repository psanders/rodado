# Where ideas come from

An idea without a concrete source doesn't get pitched. Work the list in this order every day:

1. **Inbox** (`inbox.md`), always first. Pedro's sparks, client questions, screenshots. Anything older than 14 days that was never pitched: pitch it or drop it with a note.
2. **Today's rotation** (below): 2 sources, by weekday.
3. **Bank gaps**: the slots with the fewest ideas decide which source to push harder.

## The sources

**Ad watch** — what DR product brands are running on Meta right now. Search the Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library, country Dominican Republic) or the web for brands in our categories: how many active ads, how old, are they just product photos, same creative for months? Turns into: proof ("this is what that ad could look like"), strategy ("why your ad dies in 3 weeks"), category. Never name or show the real brand in the post; the insight is the idea.

**DR calendar** — dates that make brands need new ads 2–4 weeks ahead: Día de los Reyes (Jan 6), Carnaval (Feb), Independencia (Feb 27), Semana Santa, Día de las Madres (last Sunday of May), Día de los Padres (last Sunday of July), back to school (Aug), Black Friday (late Nov), Navidad. Pitch 3–5 weeks before the date. Turns into: offer, category ("how we'd sell a Mother's Day gift set in 15 s"), strategy ("your Christmas ad should be ready in October").

**Manager questions** — what marketing managers ask, here or elsewhere: Pedro's WhatsApp and DMs (from the inbox), r/FacebookAds, r/PPC, Meta's own help forums, LinkedIn posts by LATAM marketers. Pick questions a DR manager would ask their boss or their agency. Turns into: carousel-faq, carousel-framework.

**Meta changes** — what changed in how Meta ads work: new placements, Advantage+ creative, Reels ad specs, creative fatigue and the "creative is the targeting" shift, safe zones. Search Meta for Business news and reputable ad-industry press from the last 30 days. Turns into: strategy. Explain it for a manager, not a media buyer.

**Category scan** — one DR product category at a time (coffee, rum, beer, chocolate, snacks, skincare, hair care, cleaning, pharmacy, supermarkets' own brands, bakery, dairy, juice). What would a 15 s ad for an invented brand in it look like? What's the category's cliché to avoid? Turns into: reel-category. Keep a running list in `inbox.md` of categories already used.

**Production notes** — what happened while making Rodado's ads: a cut that held attention, a prompt that failed, a client revision, how long a step took. Ask Pedro in the pitch reply if you have nothing recent ("Anything from this week's production?"). Turns into: proof, strategy, open.

**Objections** — the 3 standing objections (AI looks fake · my GM won't approve AI · US$299 is too cheap to be good) plus any new one from the inbox. Find a fresh angle each time: a comparison, a number, a before/after, a myth. Turns into: trust, open, offer.

**Craft & format** — how the best short ads and creative-strategy accounts teach (formats, not content): a carousel structure, a Reel opening, a way of showing before/after. Borrow the shape, never the words. Turns into: any slot; say which account inspired the format in the source line.

**Our own results** — what Rodado's posts did: Pedro shares numbers or screenshots (in the inbox or the Sunday reply). The best post's angle gets a follow-up; the most-asked comment becomes a post. Turns into: whatever won.

## Notes
- Record the exact link or detail in the brief's source line, so Pedro can check it.
- If a source gives nothing today, say nothing about it; use the next one.
- Don't copy another account's post. Same insight, our own angle, our own words.
