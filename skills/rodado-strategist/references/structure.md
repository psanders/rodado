# What we're building

## Audience
Marketing managers at mid-size Dominican product brands (food & drinks, beauty & personal care, consumer goods, retail) that spend US$800–5,000 a month on marketing overall. They report to a GM or owner and are judged on Meta sales/ROAS, on time, on brand, no surprises.
Not: solo sellers, giant brands with agencies. We never make content for agencies.

They must believe, in this order:
1. It looks premium and it's their real product.
2. Rodado delivers on time, every time.
3. Their boss will approve it.

Objections: "AI looks fake", "my GM won't approve AI", "US$299 seems too cheap to be good".

## Goal
Qualified WhatsApp conversations (a brand already running Meta ads, US$800+/month budget). Keyword ANUNCIO.
Rank ideas by: would it start a chat > would a manager save/share it > reach.

## Offer (facts, never change)
US$299 per ad · one story in 3 durations (30, 20, 15 s) · 2 creative directions in 48 h · 7 business days · 2 revision rounds · 100% guarantee · built from photos of the real product, no shoot.

## Weekly slots (fixed)
| Slot | Format id | Pillar | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| mon | reel-storyboard | proof | Show the work: storyboard → finished ad |
| tue | carousel-framework | strategy | A Meta creative idea managers save |
| wed | reel-category | category | "How we'd sell <DR category> in 15 s", invented brand |
| thu | carousel-faq (or carousel-framework) | trust | Remove "will my boss approve it?" |
| fri | reel-storyboard | proof | Show the work, different category than Monday |
| sat | static-quote | open | One strong opinion, for comments and shares |
| sun | reel-cta | offer | Best cut + direct ask for the WhatsApp chat |

Pillar questions: proof = "does it look premium?" · strategy = "do they understand Meta?" · category = "could this work for my product?" · trust = "will my boss approve it?" · offer = "how do I start?" · open = anything that sparks a reaction.

Format notes:
- reel-storyboard needs real project material (Pedro has it: Guaraguao, Cacao Mae, CeRa; logos replaced).
- reel-category needs only a category + an invented brand; Rodado generates it.
- carousels and static-quote need only words; the cheapest to make.
- reel-cta reuses the best cut of the week's work.

## Hard rules
- Invented brands for category ideas. Client work only with logo replaced.
- Never promise results ("vende más garantizado"), never trash a competitor or a real brand.
