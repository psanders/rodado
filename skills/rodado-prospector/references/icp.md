# Who looks like Rodado's customer

**Company:** a Dominican product brand (food & drinks, beauty & personal care, consumer goods, retail with physical products) with a marketing budget of roughly US$800–5,000/month.

## Strong signals (need at least 2)
- Active on Instagram/Facebook with **promotional posts** (launches, promos, product shots) at least weekly: a sign they also run ads. (Pedro confirms actual ads with the Ad Library link.)
- Its creatives are mostly **static product photos or repeat for months** (they need fresh creatives).
- Sold in **supermarket chains** (Sirena, Nacional, Jumbo, Bravo, Plaza Lama, La Cadena) or pharmacies (Carol, GBC), or has its own e-commerce.
- Has a **marketing role** (marketing manager, brand manager, coordinadora de mercadeo) visible on LinkedIn or its site.
- A **trigger**: new product launch, new presentation/packaging, new chain listing, a seasonal date coming (Madres, Navidad, Black Friday).

## Not a fit (skip)
- Solo sellers or tiny shops with no ads and no retail presence.
- Big multinationals or brands clearly run by a large agency (dozens of polished video ads, global campaigns).
- Agencies (we don't sell to them through outbound), restaurants, services without a physical product, real-estate, politics, alcohol brands that can't advertise on Meta in that format.
- Anyone already in `leads.json` or on the do-not-contact list.

## Score (write it in `why`)
Fit 1–3: 3 = several strong signals + a trigger; 2 = 2 signals; 1 = borderline (only propose if the day has fewer than 5).
