# Where to look (rotate; note the source in each lead)

1. **Meta Ad Library** — facebook.com/ads/library, country Dominican Republic, ad category "All ads", search category words (café, salami, galletas, ron, shampoo, crema, jugo, chocolate, snacks, detergente, panadería…). Look for brands with several active ads; open their page to see ad count, age and style. This is the best source: it proves they already pay for Meta ads.
2. **Supermarket online catalogs** — Sirena (sirena.do), Nacional (supermercadosnacional.com), Jumbo (jumbo.com.do), Bravo, Plaza Lama. Browse a category, list the local (non-multinational) brands, then check each one's Instagram and ad library.
3. **Instagram** — brand profiles found above: bio, contact button (email), latest posts and ads; also hashtags like #HechoEnRD, #MarcaDominicana, #ProductoDominicano.
4. **Company websites** — "Contacto", "Nosotros", "Trabaja con nosotros", footer; press/media pages often list a marketing email.
5. **Business news (triggers)** — Diario Libre (Economía), Listín Diario (Economía), El Dinero, Mercado: product launches, new plants, expansions, rebrands.
6. **LinkedIn** — only to learn the marketing person's name and role for the greeting; never to collect emails.
7. **Directories and associations** — Páginas Amarillas RD, AIRD (industry association), ProDominicana exporters directory, trade-fair exhibitor lists (e.g. food expos).

Rules: public business information only. Write down the exact URL where you found the email (`email_source`). If a site says it doesn't want commercial emails, skip it.
