# Where to look (rotate; note the source in each lead)

The Meta Ad Library is not available to you (Meta's tools only accept approved apps, and its API has no commercial ads outside the EU). Don't scrape it. Instead, for every lead add the Ad Library link so Pedro can check their ads in one tap before approving:
`https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=DO&search_type=keyword_unordered&q=<brand name, URL-encoded>`
Signals you *can* see: the brand posts sponsored-looking creatives on Instagram, runs promos/launches, appears in supermarket catalogs, has a marketing team.


1. **Supermarket online catalogs** — Sirena (sirena.do), Nacional (supermercadosnacional.com), Jumbo (jumbo.com.do), Bravo, Plaza Lama. Browse a category, list the local (non-multinational) brands, then check each one's Instagram and ad library.
2. **Instagram** — brand profiles found above: bio, contact button (email), latest posts; also hashtags like #HechoEnRD, #MarcaDominicana, #ProductoDominicano.
3. **Company websites** — "Contacto", "Nosotros", "Trabaja con nosotros", footer; press/media pages often list a marketing email.
4. **Business news (triggers)** — Diario Libre (Economía), Listín Diario (Economía), El Dinero, Mercado: product launches, new plants, expansions, rebrands.
5. **LinkedIn** — only to learn the marketing person's name and role for the greeting; never to collect emails.
6. **Directories and associations** — Páginas Amarillas RD, AIRD (industry association), ProDominicana exporters directory, trade-fair exhibitor lists (e.g. food expos).

Rules: public business information only. Write down the exact URL where you found the email (`email_source`). If a site says it doesn't want commercial emails, skip it.
